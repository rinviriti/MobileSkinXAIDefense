# Artifact rights and privacy

No dataset directories/archives, raw clinical photographs (including base64 encodings), patient records, private group metadata or trained weights are distributed. Original raw parity fixtures are supplied separately by authorized users. Golden-reference hashes and numeric outputs contain no trained parameter arrays.

The single PAD training illustration grid and six genuine offline Grad-CAM panels are the previously reviewed, de-identified, metadata-stripped publication figures. These composite derivatives retain PAD-UFES-20 attribution under CC BY 4.0: Pacheco et al., Data in Brief 32 (2020), 106221, DOI 10.1016/j.dib.2020.106221. Source: https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/zr7vgbcyr2/1 . They are approved figures, not a raw dataset or patient manifest.

ISIC datasets/optional private engineering fixtures remain subject to their original terms, including ISIC 2019 CC BY-NC 4.0 and original contributor attribution (BCN/Hospital Clínic de Barcelona, HAM10000/ViDIR Medical University of Vienna, MSK). Obtain permission/attribution details from https://challenge.isic-archive.com/data/ . No ISIC raw image is embedded in this repository. Dataset preparation source also covers ISIC 2020; obtain its own current publisher license before use.

Libjpeg-turbo/Pillow-derived code retains its upstream notices in the native package. No blanket project-wide permissive license is invented. Historical DATA_AND_ARTIFACT_LICENSES.md describes the earlier model-inclusive package and is not a list of binaries included here. Private GitHub visibility does not waive these restrictions.
